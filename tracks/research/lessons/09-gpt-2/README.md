# Module 09 - GPT-2: the tokenizer and the real weights

Module 08 built a GPT and trained it on Shakespeare a character at a time; this module closes the gap between that model and a published one: the same tokens in, the same numbers out. First the vocabulary. Characters make a window short, and whole words leave out every word the training text never had, 15.9% of the test text's; byte pair encoding, Sennrich et al.'s middle way, starts from the 256 values of a byte and makes the commonest pair of neighbours a new symbol again and again, so no text in any script is unknown, and 08.04's GPT on its tokens scores 2.691 bits per character against 3.332 on characters in the same steps. Then GPT-2's own tokenizer: OpenAI's vocabulary and merges read into the same code, and GPT-2's pattern for cutting text, whose Unicode letters and numbers Python's `re` cannot name, built from the standard library's tables. Against tiktoken the ids are the same on everything tried, except a letter newer than the learner's Python's Unicode tables followed by a contraction. Then GPT-2's published weights, renamed and transposed into module 08's GPT, give the reference's logits to the last bit, and one square weight left the wrong way round loads without a word and ruins the model. On the real model Holtzman et al.'s measurements come out closer to theirs than on characters: greedy loops, a low temperature brings loops back, and over 200 tokens pure sampling writes text the model finds less likely than the real text; their nucleus is not the closest to it. Last, Eldan and Li's TinyStories: a GPT of 4.3 million weights, pretrained for 45 minutes of a laptop's CPU on 64 MB of children's stories, writes whole stories of several paragraphs that end on their own without copying its training text, and predicts held-out stories better than GPT-2 small; consistent they are not. It stands on module 02's safetensors files, module 07's text and scores, and module 08's GPT, its loop and its sampler. After it, every later experiment can start from real weights, and the track has a model it pretrained that tells a story.
