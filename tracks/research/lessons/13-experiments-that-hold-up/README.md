# Module 13 - Experiments that hold up

This module takes results the track has already reported and tries to break them, with the tools a reviewer would use, and says plainly what held. 03.03's tie between Adam and SGD with Nesterov momentum is run again with a hypothesis and the rule that decides it written down before any run: with Adam's learning rate searched and SGD's taken from PyTorch's documentation, Adam wins by 8.37 points of test error, t = +21.1; given the same search, chosen on validation and reported on fresh seeds, SGD is 0.81 points behind, a tie by the rule, and a larger search run on one seed picks a rate for SGD that falls apart on fresh seeds. 08.02's four heads against one, a little beyond the noise at three seeds, is decided: four heads are better by 0.025 bits per character at ten seeds a side, lower on all ten. The lab gains standard errors, Student's t and its intervals, the bootstrap (too narrow at three seeds), paired runs (08.02's were paired all along, and paired, its three seeds had already decided it), the number of seeds a difference needs, and the test text's own noise beside the seeds'. Then the track's evaluations are attacked: the best of 32 runs chosen by half of MNIST's test set looks 1.11 points better than the average run there and is 0.78 better on the other half; MNIST and CIFAR-10 hold no exact copies of their test images, but CIFAR-10 has 346 with a twin in training by a stated rule, worth 2.0 points to a classifier that only remembers; Tiny Shakespeare's split shares no run of 32 characters, and no held-out TinyStory the track scores is in its training stories. Last, Kaplan et al.'s scaling law on the track's own GPT, the module's recorded long run of 51 minutes: five widths predicted a sixth four times larger at 3.904 bits per token, between 3.836 and 3.973, before it finished training, and it scored 3.925; counted with the embeddings the same runs missed by 3.2%, the paper's reason for leaving them out; the exponent, 0.097 against the paper's 0.076, is for models stopped far from convergence, not the paper's measurement. It stands on module 00's seeds and Welch's t, module 03's optimisers, module 05's held-out sets, module 08's GPT and module 09's TinyStories. After it a result in the track comes with its hypothesis and rule fixed first, a baseline searched as hard as the method, an interval and the seeds it needed, an evaluation checked for luck and for leaks, and, for a claim about size, a prediction made before the run.
