# Module 10 - Today's architecture

Module 09 left a GPT that holds GPT-2's published weights; the open models released since are built from the same blocks with four pieces changed, and this module makes each change in module 08's GPT, one paper at a time, against the piece it replaced, at the same size and budget and over several seeds. RMSNorm, Zhang and Sennrich's layer norm without the mean, gives the same bits, 3.561 against 3.563, and on this laptop's CPU a slower step: PyTorch's layer norm is one fused kernel, and written out alike RMSNorm takes about two thirds of layer norm's time; in the trained model the mean step was doing almost nothing. Rotary positions, Su et al.'s, turn each query and key through angles that grow with its place, so a score depends only on how far apart two places are, exactly; they are worth 0.315 bits over learned positions at the page's budget, read windows longer than any they trained on though worse there, and make a step slower, because the angles are made again on every call. A gated MLP, Shazeer's SwiGLU, is behind after a minute of training and ahead once trained a while. Grouped-query attention, Ainslie et al.'s, lets query heads share key and value heads: half as many cost nothing measurable. Then a model of today with all four, SmolLM2-135M, loads into the learner's GPT and gives the reference's logits to the last bit, with its tokenizer, which cuts every digit alone and has no symbol for 21 bytes; it scores 1.405 bits per character on Shakespeare's test text, a text it may have seen. Last, the ablation table, every change alone and all four together on one footing: the page's short run ranks rotary far ahead and SwiGLU behind; the module's recorded long run of 42 minutes makes SwiGLU the largest single gain and rotary the next, both beyond the noise, RMSNorm and grouped heads inside it, and all four together the lowest row with 10% fewer weights. It stands on module 04's layer norm, module 08's GPT, its loop and its sampler, and module 09's tokenizer and loading. After it the learner can open the source of a model released this year and find nothing in it they have not written.
