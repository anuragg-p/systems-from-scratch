# Module 11 - Fast

Module 10 left a GPT built as open models are built now; this module makes the same numbers come sooner, every change checked to give the same numbers and measured before and after, with the machine's load beside every time. The yardstick comes first: a time is a spread, so the first step is set aside, the same work is timed again and again, and the smallest of several, which moves least with the load, is kept beside the median; and the measure is the time until held-out bits reach a target, by which half the batch, a third shorter a step and 52% more steps, arrives no sooner. A profile of one step, with plain timers and PyTorch's profiler, corrects what module 10 believed: rotary's angles are 0.5% of a step and keeping them saves nothing, while the turning is 15.8% and the largest piece is attention written out, 128 small matrices a block taken one at a time, forward and backward. PyTorch's fused attention, which works as Dao et al.'s FlashAttention does, gives the same numbers to 2.7e-15, a training step 1.29 to 1.42 times faster on this CPU and memory kept for the backward pass that grows with the window instead of its square; on the Apple GPU, nothing. Sixteen bits: every fact Micikevicius et al. give about what float16 and bfloat16 cannot hold held on this model's own numbers, a quarter of the late updates lost in float16 and most in bfloat16, Adam's eps 0, loss scaling needed; the speed did not, since this build of PyTorch has no fast 16-bit products on the CPU and a step is 8 to 29 times slower, and autocast found a bug in 10.02's angles, fixed. Gradient accumulation gives a larger batch's gradient exactly and no speed; tokens a second grow 3.4 times from 8 windows to 128 while the time to a target barely moves, and the clip cuts nearly every early step. Last, one more change chosen from a profile, the profile of writing text: a cache of keys and values writes the same tokens 2.8 to 6.3 times as fast on SmolLM2, keeping a third as much with its grouped heads; and the module's recorded benchmark puts every row on one table, fused attention 13% less time to 2.4 bits, all together 14%, bfloat16 29 times more. It stands on module 00's spread and Welch's t, 06.02's clock, module 08's GPT and its loop, and module 10's architecture and SmolLM2. After it the learner can say where a step's time goes on their own machine, and make a run sooner without changing what it computes.
