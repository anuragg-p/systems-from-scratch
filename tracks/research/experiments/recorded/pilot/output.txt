1. The question: How much are learned positions worth to a GPT as blocks are added, and how many blocks does a GPT without positions need before it matches one with them, at the size and budget of 08.03, on Tiny Shakespeare?
   It comes from 08.03: at 4 blocks, 3.407 bits per character without positions against 3.342 with them, a gap of 0.065.
   The predictions, written down before any run, and the rule that decides each:
   one_block            Positions are worth the most to a GPT of one block, which cannot see order at all without them.
                        (held if the mean gap at 1 block is the largest)
   shrinks              The gap shrinks as blocks are added: the mask gives a deeper model more of the order.
                        (held if the mean gap falls at every step of depth)
   size_one             At one block the gap is more than 0.1 bits per character.
                        (held if the gap's interval at 1 is all above 0.1)
   again                At four blocks, each arm with its own searched rate, the gap is about 08.03's 0.065.
                        (held if the mean gap at 4 is within 0.0325 to 0.13)
   not_closed           No depth tried closes the gap: at the deepest, positions still help beyond the noise.
                        (held if the gap's interval at deepest is all above 0)
   depth_no_substitute  Twice the blocks do not buy what positions buy: no positions at 8 blocks is worse than learned positions at 4.
                        (held if Welch's interval (none at deepest, learned at 4) is above 0)
   not_weights          The positions' weights are not why they help: the same number of weights given to the MLPs of a GPT without positions closes under a quarter of the gap.
                        (held if it closes under 25% of the gap at 4)
