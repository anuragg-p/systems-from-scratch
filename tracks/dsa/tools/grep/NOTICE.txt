What the top-level NOTICE should say of grep, in the dsa section:

grep (tools/grep/, lib/strsearch.c, lib/re.c, lib/nfa.c, lib/dfa.c)

Upstream   None. Written from POSIX's definition of extended regular
           expressions and from the published algorithms: Horspool's
           search (1980), Thompson's construction (1968), and a DFA
           built as it runs, as Russ Cox describes RE2's. Checked
           against the machine's grep: GNU grep 3.11 on Linux
           (https://www.gnu.org/software/grep/, GPL-3.0-or-later; not
           part of GNU coreutils, which the dsa section names) and
           macOS's BSD grep 2.6.0-FreeBSD (BSD-2-Clause). No code from
           any of them is in the track.
Data       None new: grep runs on data/book.txt and on the track's own
           source; tools/grep/gen.py (ours) makes the rest.
