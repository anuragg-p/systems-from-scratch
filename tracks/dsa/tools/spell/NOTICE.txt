tools/spell/words.txt - the dictionary spell reads unless -d names another

What it is
  The 2of4brif list of Alan Beale's 12dicts word lists: 60388 English
  words, one to a line. British and international spelling (-ise and -ize
  both), with their inflections (plurals, verb forms); no proper names,
  abbreviations, hyphenated words, contractions or words of one letter.
  Lowercase a-z only, sorted byte by byte (LC_ALL=C sort), LF line ends,
  all ASCII. 563527 bytes.

Licence, as read
  The 12dicts readme (version 6.0.2, its last paragraph; the same text is
  alt12dicts/README-orig in the repository above, and the readme page at
  http://wordlist.aspell.net/12dicts-readme/):

    The 12dicts lists were compiled by Alan Beale. I explicitly release
    them to the public domain, but request acknowledgment of their use.
    (Actually, the dependency of the 2of12inf list and the 2+2+3 lists on
    AGID prevents their release into the public domain. However, I do not
    impose any additional requirements on their use beyond those imposed
    by AGID and its sources, as described in agid.txt.)

  2of4brif is neither 2of12inf nor one of the 2+2+3 lists, so it is in
  the public domain, with no condition. The alt12dicts README says the
  same of all its files ("All of these files have been explicitly placed
  in the Public Domain by Alan Beale."), and SCOWL's Copyright file
  (scowl/Copyright in the same repository), which takes 2of4brif in at
  its level 55, says: "Like his other stuff the 2of4brif is also in the
  public domain."

  So it can go into this MIT-licensed repository as it is. Nothing is
  required; acknowledgment is asked for, and this is it: the word list is
  the work of Alan Beale, from his 12dicts lists. Thank you.

Changes made
  None to its contents: words.txt is byte for byte the file fetched (the
  hashes above are of both). It was renamed from 2of4brif.txt.

  spell checks words of two letters or more only (as aspell does by
  default), because the list has no words of one letter: "a" and "I" are
  not in it. That is in spell.c, not a change to the list.
